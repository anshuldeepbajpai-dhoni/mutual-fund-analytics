# Advanced Analytics Insights

## 1. Historical VaR and CVaR Analysis

The Value at Risk (VaR) analysis identified funds with the highest downside exposure under normal market conditions. Funds with more negative VaR values indicate greater potential daily losses at the 95% confidence level. Similarly, Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR) highlighted the average losses during extreme market movements, helping investors understand tail-risk exposure beyond the VaR threshold.

## 2. Rolling Sharpe Ratio Trends

The 90-day rolling Sharpe Ratio analysis revealed differences in risk-adjusted performance across the selected mutual funds. Certain schemes maintained consistently positive Sharpe ratios, indicating stable returns relative to volatility, while others experienced significant fluctuations during changing market conditions.

## 3. Investor Cohort Analysis

Investor cohorts were formed based on the year of their first investment transaction. Recent cohorts contributed a substantial portion of total investments, reflecting growing retail participation in mutual funds. The analysis also identified the most preferred schemes within each cohort, providing insights into changing investment preferences over time.

## 4. SIP Continuity and At-Risk Investors

The SIP continuity analysis examined investors with at least six SIP transactions. Investors whose average gap between consecutive SIP investments exceeded 35 days were classified as "At-Risk." This metric helps identify potential SIP discontinuation trends and supports targeted investor engagement strategies.

## 5. Portfolio Concentration Using HHI

The Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) was used to measure portfolio concentration across equity funds. Funds with higher HHI values exhibit greater concentration in a limited number of holdings or sectors, increasing concentration risk. Lower HHI values indicate better diversification and reduced exposure to individual sectors or securities.

## Conclusion

The advanced analytics framework provides a comprehensive assessment of mutual fund risk, investor behavior, portfolio diversification, and recommendation strategies. The findings can support better investment decisions, improve investor retention, and enhance the overall understanding of fund performance under different market conditions.
